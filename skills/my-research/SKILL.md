# my-research

Explore options and product possibilities before narrowing. Verify uncertain/current facts using primary sources; cite evidence and distinguish inference. Compare benefits, costs and tradeoffs, then recommend. Stop once evidence supports the decision. Do not implement during research. See [research workflow](references/research.md).
