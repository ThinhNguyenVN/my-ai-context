# my-risk-based-verification

Read [verification workflow](references/verification.md). Choose checks for concrete risks; no mandatory baseline or full suite. Ask before expensive/broad checks. Repeat only for new changes, failures or unresolved risks. Relevant platforms may be desktop/mobile web, iOS and Android; do not claim all verified from one pass.
